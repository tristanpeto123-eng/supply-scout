What the agent learns next

  1. Verify whether scarcity reflects actual supply limits rather than generic search matches.
  2. Watch changes in independent evidence over multiple collection dates.
  3. Map producers, equipment suppliers and affected customers; check both benefits and cost exposure.
  4. Compare forward 90-day stock returns against a currency-appropriate benchmark, after costs.
  5. Score new candidates only after a leakage-controlled chronological holdout and paper testing.
